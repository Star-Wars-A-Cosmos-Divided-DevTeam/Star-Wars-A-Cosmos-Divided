# Product Context

## Why This Project Exists
The Star Wars A Cosmos Divided mod exists to bring the rich universe of Star Wars into the space strategy game Cosmoteer. Players who enjoy both Star Wars and Cosmoteer can now experience their favorite universe in a new strategic context.

## Problems This Project Solves
- Lack of Star Wars themed content in Cosmoteer
- Limited strategic options for players who want to engage with Star Wars lore
- Absence of authentic Star Wars faction and ship designs in the game

## How It Should Work
The mod should seamlessly integrate with Cosmoteer's existing systems while providing authentic Star Wars themed content. Players should be able to:
- Build and customize ships using Star Wars themed components
- Engage in strategic battles using Star Wars weapons and systems
- Experience the unique mechanics and lore of various Star Wars factions
- Enjoy visual and thematic consistency with the Star Wars universe

## User Experience Goals
- Intuitive integration with existing Cosmoteer gameplay
- Authentic Star Wars aesthetics and theming
- Balanced gameplay mechanics that don't break existing game balance
- Clear visual distinction for Star Wars elements
- Easy to understand faction and ship relationships
