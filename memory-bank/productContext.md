# Product Context

## Why This Mod Exists
- The Star Wars A Cosmos Divided mod exists to bring the rich universe of Star Wars into the space strategy game Cosmoteer. Players who enjoy both Star Wars and Cosmoteer can now experience their favorite universe in a new strategic context.
- Cosmoteer players want an immersive Star Wars kit with iconic weapons, droids, and ships.
- Vanilla progression lacks a Star Wars-flavored resource loop; the mod introduces hypermatter, durasteel, doonium, and related tech tiers.

## Users & Scenarios
- Ship designers building and customizing lore-accurate Star Wars craft using themed components and styles in the blueprint editor / ship builder. 
- Career players seeking Star Wars themed, with new mining/refining loops, tech unlocks, and asteroid deposits.
- PvP builders relying on curated build-battle tech lists that include Star Wars parts.

## Experience Goals
- Familiar Star Wars assets accessible through intuitive editor categories and localized strings.
- Clear UI feedback (stat widgets, indicators) for mechanics such as hypermatter generation and thermal overclock states.
- Consistent audiovisual presentation using shared SW_* effect registries.
- The mod should seamlessly integrate with Cosmoteer's existing systems while providing semi-authentic Star Wars themed content.
- Intuitive integration with existing Cosmoteer gameplay
- Authentic Star Wars aesthetics and theming
- Balanced gameplay mechanics that don't break existing game balance
- Clear visual distinction for Star Wars elements

## Value Proposition
- Consolidates disparate Star Wars assets (parts, effects, sounds) into one cohesive, maintainable package.
- Keeps parity with Cosmoteer updates via explicit version targeting and non-destructive overrides of vanilla data.
